How to change this page

  1. Sign in to GitHub with your own account.
  2. In the search box at the top, type emas-solutionmanagement and press Enter. Click furyknl/emas-solutionmanagement in the results.
  3. Click your own file: kaleb-d.html.
  4. Click the pencil.
  5. GitHub asks you to make your own copy. Click Fork this repository. Expect this message and ignore it: "You need to fork this repository to propose changes. Sorry, you're not able to edit this repository directly…"
  6. Replace "This is my page. I have not changed it yet." with a sentence of your own.
  7. Click Commit changes, write a short message describing the change for future reference, then click Propose changes.
  8. Click Create pull request. A form opens with a title and a description. Then click Create pull request again, at the bottom of that form. Only this second click sends your request to the lecturer.

Your change goes live after the lecturer accepts it.

Changing your request afterwards

  1. At the top right of any GitHub page, click the Pull requests icon, between the circle and the book. Not the Pull requests tab under a repository name.
  2. Click your request. Its title is probably Update kaleb-d.html.
  3. Under the title it says "… wants to merge … from your-name:patch-1". Click that blue label.
  4. Click your own file: kaleb-d.html.
  5. Click the pencil and change your sentence.
  6. Click Commit changes…, keep Commit directly to the patch-1 branch selected, and click Commit changes.
  7. Open your request again, as in steps 1 and 2. Its Commits tab now shows one more. It is still the same request.

Do not go back to the lecturer's repository and click the pencil there again: that starts a second request instead of changing this one. To withdraw your request, open it and click Close pull request.
